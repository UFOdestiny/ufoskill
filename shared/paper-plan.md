# A compact paper plan

Use this structure for a new full paper, a substantial reorganization, or a review spanning several sections. Reuse an existing outline or planning document. Create `paper-plan.md` in the manuscript project only when a durable plan helps the authorized writing task; review alone should not silently edit the author's plan. A paragraph edit needs only the relevant claims and terms in context.

Keep the plan brief enough to consult while writing. Record unknowns explicitly instead of filling them with plausible research.

## Research argument

- **Paper type and reader:** empirical method, theory, analysis, dataset, or system; relevant community.
- **Target constraints:** venue/year/track, verified page accounting, format, manuscript language; unknown requirements remain unknown.
- **Problem:** the task and concrete consequence of the unresolved problem.
- **Closest prior work:** what it already does and the precise remaining limitation under the same assumptions.
- **Insight:** the observation or reasoning that motivates the contribution; identify whether it is measured or hypothesized.
- **Contribution:** the specific change and why it addresses that limitation.
- **Takeaway:** the strongest conclusion the available evidence supports.

## Claims and evidence

| Claim | Closest comparison or alternative explanation | Supplied evidence and location | Supported scope / unresolved gap | Main-text home |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Tie each advertised contribution to a result, proof, analysis, or artifact. Evidence can support more than one claim, but repeated claims should retain the same scope. Mark proposed experiments as proposed. If a contribution lacks evidence, narrow it or flag the gap; do not write around the absence.

Use the [introduction guide](../skills/ai-paper-writing/references/introduction.md) to map supported contributions to the paper type. Record missing evidence without adding contributions to fill a role.

## Terminology and notation

| Concept | Community term | Precise meaning here | Symbol | Easily confused terms | Definition source, if needed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Include consequential terms and overloaded symbols, not every common word. Keep the author's established method name unless a rename is authorized.

## Section plan and space

| Section | Reader question / responsibility | Claim and evidence to carry | Approximate page budget | Appendix destination |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Apply the [page-budget guidance](page-budget.md) and [appendix organization](appendix.md). Establish the section's job before drafting it. Record important dependencies: what must be understood before a theorem, method operation, or experiment can make sense.

## Carry decisions forward

After a substantial writing change, update only affected claims, terminology, and allocation decisions. A reviewer proposes corrections to the plan rather than treating it as proof of the manuscript's validity. A technical checker reports inconsistencies against actual sources. No separate tracking file is required for each skill.
